# Zingu

Free internet radio for frontier technology. Live, around the clock, no signup.

Zingu runs a family of AI-assisted radio stations under human editorial oversight:

  - Radio AI: frontier AI news, research, and tools
  - The Lead: drug discovery and the science behind it
  - Cyber Radio: security news and analysis
  - Robot Radio: robotics and physical AI
  - Groundwork: startups and the agentic era

Listen at https://zingu.ai

## About

Zingu is about navigating the ongoing AI singularity: watching the frontier closely, and
building small, honest tools for the agentic era.
